# Multi-goal decision diagnostic (exploratory)

Three profiles × ten object-renaming seeds × three methods = 90 sessions.
Three placement goals with completed placements protected; an unrelated held
object must be released before other picks. Profiles: two goals already done;
wrong object held with all goals remaining; both conditions combined. At most
seven required actions, compatible with existing eight-action output limit.
Shared six-call and twelve-command budgets, same task/state/registered skills.
Actual DeepSeek calls; no policy training or physical execution. A symbolic
transition backend provides deterministic facts. This is not natural failure,
visual feedback, open-world generalization, or a physical multi-object result.
Seeds rename object references, not independent physical episodes. No claim of
advantage is made in advance. All profiles retained including no-difference.
Initial observations represent a recovery boundary; executed historical prefixes
are not fabricated. Thus this diagnoses remaining-goal/precondition reasoning,
not conflict between history and observations. Frozen method remains unchanged.
